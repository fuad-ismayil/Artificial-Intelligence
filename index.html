Frequently Asked Questions

AI refers to machines or software that are designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving.

AI systems learn by processing large amounts of data and identifying patterns using algorithms like machine learning or deep learning.

The primary types include Narrow AI (task-specific), General AI (human-level intelligence), and Superintelligent AI (beyond human capabilities).

Most modern AI platforms use encryption and strict security protocols, but data safety depends on the provider’s policies and how responsibly the user shares sensitive information.

AI is widely used in healthcare, finance, transportation, customer support, marketing, cybersecurity, and everyday applications like smartphones and smart home devices.

AI will automate some routine tasks, but it will also create new job opportunities. Human creativity, empathy, and strategic thinking remain essential.

AI responses are based on data and patterns, so accuracy is generally high, but they can still make mistakes. Verification is recommended for critical information.

Most AI tools are designed to be user-friendly and do not require technical background, though advanced usage may benefit from basic understanding of AI concepts.
